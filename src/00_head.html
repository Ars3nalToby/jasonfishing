South-east Queensland · land-based and boat fishing

Saltline

Walk a real SEQ pier, cast a float, and fight bream, flathead, tailor and mulloway. Weather, wind and tide are live where the network allows. Every fish is checked against Queensland size and possession limits before it goes in the esky.

You start at the end of the pier with the rod out. Desktop: click to look · WASD walk · hold click or Space to cast and reel · A/D steer a running fish · B swap bait · T tackle shop · E boat. Phone: stick to walk, drag to look, big button to cast. Places are stylised, not surveyed. Boat hire prices are advertised rates checked 15 Oct 2026.
